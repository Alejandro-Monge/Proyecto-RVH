# Module — Approach occupancy of the messages shadow (`reconstruccion_causal/scripts/occupancy_core.py`)

Occupancy time series of the approach lanes to J5, computed (a) live over the reconstructed messages
shadow (`causal_player.py`) and (b) offline over the layer-0 replica (GT2) from its fcd
(`gt2_occupancy.py`). Both outputs share schema and rule and are compared downstream by the comparator.

Each side's output goes to its own file, never mixed with the dense dump:

```
sim_time_s, brazo, carril, ocupacion, detenidos
```

One row per lane and logical tick (0.1 s), with **explicit zeros** (an empty lane emits a row with 0;
a gap in the series would be ambiguous). Per approach it is the sum of its 3 lanes, aggregated
downstream, not recounted.

## Membership rule

Single rule, shared by both sides:

- `ocupacion(carril)` = number of vehicles whose `laneID` is among the arm's approach lanes.
- `detenidos(carril)` = the same ones with `speed < 0.1` m/s (strict).
- Approach lanes: **exclusively** from `config/sites/rusia/sumo/lane_map.yaml` →
  `arms.<brazo>.entry` (12 lanes: `E1_*`=west, `E3_*`=east, `E5_*`=south, `E6_*`=north; mapping by
  geometry, not by name). Never from the net, never hardcoded.
- Threshold 0.1 m/s: `config/sites/rusia/occupancy.yaml` (`halting_speed_mps`). It is
  `SUMO_const_haltingSpeed`, SUMO's native halting criterion (TraCI doc, Lane/Edge Value
  Retrieval), not a threshold of the team's own.
- A vehicle on an internal lane `:J5_*` is out **by construction** (no internal lane is in the map): it
  is crossing, not approaching.

Single implementation: `reconstruccion_causal/scripts/occupancy_core.py`, a pure function
(`occupancy_counts(states, approach_lanes, halting_speed_mps)`, with `states` = iterable of
`(vehicle_id, lane_id, speed)`). The two consumers import it; there are no copies of the rule.

## Shadow side (live hook)

`causal_player.py --occupancy-out <csv>`: computation after `simulationStep`, per logical tick (0.1 s),
**never** in the sub-steps of attach mode (there it only re-commands; there is no new state). The data
arrive by TraCI subscription (`VAR_ROAD, VAR_LANE, VAR_SPEED`), with results that come in the step
response: the hook adds no TraCI round-trips.

- **`speed` = `VAR_SPEED`**: SUMO's post-step speed, which for a vehicle commanded by
  `moveToXY` derives from the displacement and measures what the shadow **represents**. A frozen frame
  gives ~0 and counts as stopped.
- **The `speed` column of the dense dump is not used**: that column is `speed_csv` (`causal_core.py`,
  field `spd`), i.e. the speed reported by the message (that of the observed message in OBSERVED slots,
  interpolated in INFERRED/INTERPOLATED and frozen at the last message during UNCERTAIN/DR). It serves
  to audit the message content, not the represented state; using it in `detenidos` would hide the
  channel freezes.
- Eligibles: only **revealed** shadows (active, with state already applied, `last_applied` non-null,
  and outside `parked`: a parked ghost from park mode is on a real lane at v=0 and would count as
  stopped).
- Traceability: `summary.json` records the file, rows, threshold, and the **md5 of the lane_map** read
  in the run.
- Diagnostics (off by default, passive): `--occupancy-probe-out` (per tick and vehicle: lane,
  `VAR_SPEED`, eligibility) and `--sumo-fcd-out` (fcd of the own standalone SUMO, for the symmetry
  check). Passivity verified by a byte-identical `validation_log` (see Verifications).

## GT2 side (offline generator)

`gt2_occupancy.py --fcd <fcd_capa0> --config <occupancy.yaml> --out <csv>`: applies the same pure
function tick by tick over the fcd-output of the layer-0 replica (Path B chain: canonical cfg
`rusia_via_b.sumo.cfg` —no route-files, a 19-link network— plus `driver_replay.py`, GT2 mobility
`f712b746`, **62 vehicles**; the fcd is generated by an auxiliary script of the layer-0 chain, offline,
not included, a clone of the canonical script with `--fcd-output` via CLI and the pinned cfg intact).
It is independent of the message channel by construction: it reads nothing from the RSU or from the
shadow.

The fcd carries `lane` and `speed` directly per vehicle and step (default fcd-output attributes, SUMO
1.12). The `.rou` is not an input (in Path B it does not exist; the demand is injected by the driver
via TraCI). The layer-0 SUMO runs at 0.05 s; the generator **samples the timesteps that are multiples
of 0.1 s** to produce the same logical grid as the shadow.

| Parameter | Value | File | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `halting_speed_mps` | 0.1 | `config/sites/rusia/occupancy.yaml` | SUMO's native halting |

| logical grid | 0.1 s | `causal_core.TICK` / the generator's `--tick` | reconstructor tick |

No existing md5 pin was modified; the lane_map md5 is recorded per run.

## Known limitations

1. **`detenidos` in the shadow under DR-until-anchor has a systematic bias dependent on loss.**
   With the DR policy, a stopped real vehicle that loses signal keeps being extrapolated with its past
   speed: while the DR lasts **it does not count as stopped even though GT2 does count it**.
   Consequence: `ocupacion` is robust to loss (the vehicle stays in the lane the same), but
   `detenidos` in the shadow **does not equal real stops during the DR stretches**, and its bias grows
   with loss. It is a channel effect (information not available), not a counting error, and it is
   part of what the comparison against GT2 must exhibit.
2. **GT2 coverage = 62 vehicles** (70 recovered chains − 8 declared discards from a perception limit).
   Do not compare with GT1 denominators (video, 73 cross): they are distinct universes.
3. **Internal lanes out by construction** (see Membership rule): occupancy measures the approach, not
   the crossing interior.
4. **Attach mode (compared view) is not a measurement context**: with server-step 0.05 and sub-step
   re-commands, `VAR_SPEED` is distorted. The metric is produced in standalone runs (step 0.1).
   The hook computes only on logical ticks anyway.
5. **The temporal offset shadow↔GT2** (channel delay: 1 s buffer plus period quantization plus DR)
   is a measurable property of the channel: the comparator reports it as a result (time series plus
   lag analysis), never absorbed into an aggregate.
6. **The speed channel runs a step behind the position channel** (measured): `VAR_SPEED` post-step(t)
   is the previous step's speed, and `fcd(t)` is the pre-step(t) state. Consequence: the start and
   the end of a stop are recorded 1 tick (0.1 s) late in the shadow (0.05 s in GT2), a pure phase
   shift, without duration bias. It is irrelevant against the channel's ≥1 s delay;
   it is only seen when comparing series tick by tick.

## Verifications

Smoke check, case 1 Hz / P40 (`runs/occ_smoke_1Hz_P40`, guard PASS, 0 violations; 22 176
rows = 1848 ticks × 12 lanes):

- (a) real lane_map → 12 exact IDs: **PASSED**
  (`tests/test_occupancy_core.py::test_lane_map_real_produce_los_12_ids_exactos`; full suite
  12/12).
- (b) frozen ⇒ stopped: **3952/3975 frozen ticks with `VAR_SPEED < 0.1`** (99.42 %). The 23
  exceptions are all the first tick of their frozen run (23/23): it is the one-step lag of the speed
  channel (limitation 6), not a counting error. From the second tick of the run, 0
  exceptions.
- (c) revealed without spurious speed: **62/62 creations and 17/17 reacquisitions read `VAR_SPEED = 0.000`**
  on their first eligible tick. That implies that the first tick of a revealed moving vehicle counts
  as stopped for 1 tick (≤79 vehicle-ticks in the whole run, the same phenomenon as
  limitation 6).
- (d) instrumental symmetry probe↔fcd of the same run: **max|ΔV| = 0.005 m/s** (= the fcd's 2-decimal
  quantization) when aligning probe(t) ↔ fcd(t−0.1); at lag 0, max|ΔV| = 16.04. The grid off-by-one
  exists, is detected and calibrated (limitation 6). Both sides measure the same physical
  quantity.
- (e) passivity, root determinism criterion: **byte-identical with sorted output, without
  PYTHONHASHSEED or any environment variable**. The player iterates in sorted order over every set
  whose effects touch an output and orders by contract the `validation_log` (ascending tick,
  vehicle_id) and the dense one on writing. Certified: `validation_log`, `summary.json`, and
  `crossing_times.csv` byte-identical between two identical runs; A'-vs-C' and B'-vs-C' byte-identical
  (full instrumentation / occupancy only / nothing); dense-vs-C' byte-identical; occupancy series
  A'==B'. The md5 of the sorted content is identical to that of `cert_matrix_vigente_ref` (1Hz/P40
  `1826d307…`, 2Hz/P00 `d71ba5d9…`): the 3003 MC runs remain valid without re-executing.

GT2 (step 0, generated offline by the auxiliary script not included): the layer-0 fcd in
`reconstruccion_causal/inputs/fcd_capa0_gt2.xml` — 62 ids = exactly those of the manifest (`v*`),
uniform 0.05 s grid, t ∈ [0, 189.95], 3800 timesteps, 5.4 MB; chain 62/62 inserted, 62/62
arrived, err_moveto=0.

## Occupancy comparator (messages)

- **Source-agnostic core**: `scripts/occupancy_compare.py` — `compare_series(sombra, gt)`
  over the common schema; it serves vision equally when its series adapter exists (not yet
  built). Messages consumer:
  `reconstruccion_causal/scripts/comparar_ocupacion_mensajes.py`.
- **Grid control** (`verify_grid`, aborts with `GridMismatch`): single 0.1 step, same phase,
  same 12 keys, contiguous intersection; edge trimming is declared in `grid_report`, with no
  silent truncation. Verified with data over the real pairs (P00: 1896 common ticks, 31
  shadow-only at the end declared; P40: 1848 common, 52 GT-only).
- **Option (c)**: aligned series (figure input; the render belongs to the figures task), MAE(lag)
  curve with GT shifted lag ∈ [0,3] step 0.1, and always the triple (MAE@0, lag*, MAE@lag*) plus
  delta = MAE@0 − MAE@lag*. **lag\* only at the approach level; the lanes inherit it** (counts 0-5 do
  not optimize their own argmin). MC aggregation: median curve first and lag* of the aggregated
  argmin, never averaging lag* per rep.
- **Detenidos enters with a caveat in the output**: a `nota` column in each detenidos row of the
  summary plus a `salvedad_detenidos` field in the JSON (the DR bias of limitation 1: it does not
  equal real stops, it grows with loss). It is impossible to read occupancy and detenidos as
  equivalent from the artifact itself.
- **Closing verification**: positive control GT-vs-GT → MAE@0=0, lag*=0, exact=1.0 (8/8
  approach×metric) PASS; byte determinism (two runs → 4/4 identical files) PASS.
- **First readings** (per approach, occupancy): 2 Hz/P00 — **lag\* = 1.1 s uniform across the 4 approaches**
  (pure channel delay, consistent with the 7.2 delay of +1.066 s; P00 separates delay from loss),
  MAE@lag* 0.013–0.254 (exact@lag* 0.97–0.99 in east/west/south; north 0.75). 1 Hz/P40 — the MAE rises
  (0.20–0.76), lag* disperses (0.7–1.3) and delta shrinks (north 0.002: it is no longer an offset, it
  is a counting error); detenidos degrades more than occupancy, the DR bias of limitation 1 measured.
  Artifacts: `runs/occ_2Hz_P00/comparacion/` and `runs/occ_smoke_1Hz_P40/comparacion/`.

## Occupancy results

Tabular result metric (not a figure), generated deterministically from the comparator's pinned
outputs by `scripts/paper_figures_resultados/tabla_ocupacion_fidelidad.py` →
`verify_out/tablas_resultados/tabla_ocupacion_fidelidad.csv` (plus md5 seal). Aggregate weighting
**declared**: mean over approaches weighted by the approach's mean (GT) occupancy; **the lag\* is not
averaged** (comparator rule), its range across approaches is reported.

**2 Hz/P00 (clean base), per approach:**

| approach | mean occ (GT) | MAE@0 | lag\* (s) | MAE@lag\* | delta | exact@lag\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| east  | 1.66 | 0.206 | 1.1 | 0.025 | 0.180 | 0.975 |
| north | 1.18 | 0.336 | 1.1 | **0.254** | 0.082 | **0.746** |
| west | 1.01 | 0.123 | 1.1 | 0.025 | 0.098 | 0.975 |
| south   | 1.43 | 0.089 | 1.1 | 0.013 | 0.075 | 0.987 |
| **aggregate** (wtd.) | — | 0.187 | **[1.1, 1.1]** | 0.073 | 0.114 | — |

**1 Hz/P40 (stress, 40 % loss), per approach:**

| approach | mean occ (GT) | MAE@0 | lag\* (s) | MAE@lag\* | delta | exact@lag\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| east  | 1.70 | 0.762 | 1.1 | 0.647 | 0.115 | 0.390 |
| north | 1.21 | 0.644 | 0.7 | 0.643 | 0.002 | 0.574 |
| west | 1.04 | 0.314 | 1.3 | 0.229 | 0.085 | 0.804 |
| south   | 1.47 | 0.201 | 1.1 | 0.156 | 0.044 | 0.848 |
| **aggregate** (wtd.) | — | 0.498 | **[0.7, 1.3]** | 0.433 | 0.065 | — |

**Uniform delay and convergence with metric 7.2.** In the clean base the lag\* is **1.1 s in the four
approaches** (range [1.1, 1.1]): the offset of the occupancy series is pure channel delay and
coincides with the crossing delay 7.2 (+1.066 s, measured by matching) within the lag grid
(0.1 s), so two independent instruments fall in the ~1 s buffer. Under heavy loss
(P40) the lag\* disperses to [0.7, 1.3] and the delta shrinks (north 0.002: the degradation is no
longer an offset but a counting error); the lag window stops being well defined when the series
degrades.

**Recommended reporting level: per-approach breakdown, not just the aggregate.** In the clean base
three approaches give MAE@lag\* ~0.01–0.03 (exact@lag\* 0.97–0.99), but **north deviates 10×**
(MAE@lag\* 0.254, exact@lag\* 0.746). The weighted aggregate (0.073) hides that residue; north's worst
fit is in itself a result (a far-field approach / north entry, already problematic in perception),
not noise to be averaged. The paper table is the per-approach rows of 2 Hz/P00 plus one aggregate
row; 1 Hz/P40 provides the stress rows that show the degradation under loss. The aggregate is only
defensible for the headline ("occupancy MAE ~0.07 veh after correcting the channel delay in the clean
base"), with the breakdown supporting it.

**Validity: the messages occupancy is clean (not in-sample).** Unlike the visual occupancy
—whose instrument tunes (dwell θ, gap-merging g) over the same 180 s clip that it evaluates,
in-sample, declared "not paper" in `measure_occupancy_accuracy.py:1-5`—, the messages occupancy
**has no tuned parameter**: it is the direct lane-membership rule
(`occupancy_core.occupancy_counts`, a single implementation shared by the live hook and GT2).
The only threshold is the stopping one **0.1 m/s = SUMO's native halting criterion** (a config
constant, not tuned). The **GT2 comes from the `fcd-output` of the layer-0 replica** (the Path B
reality), **independent of the message channel by construction** ("reads no RSU CSV or anything from
the shadow", `gt2_occupancy.py`). Without self-referential tuning, the numbers are a clean and
reportable measurement. This is what makes the messages occupancy publishable and not the visual one.

**Presentation in the paper: table, not figure.** The occupancy result is this table (MAE@0 →
MAE@lag\* per approach). The explored figures —an overlaid GT vs shadow series (figR03a) and an
MAE(lag) curve (figR03b)— are **discarded from the paper** with a traceable reason: the series does
not measure (it is illustrative and is only read in a chosen window or approach, with a
cherry-picking risk; the ~1 s offset is invisible over the 190 s), and the MAE(lag) curve is
redundant with the table's `lag\*` column. Their files are kept as support, outside the paper
(inventory in `verify_out/figuras_resultados/README.md`). The visual occupancy does not enter as a
result (in-sample instrument); it is mentioned in text as a **capability** (injection bridge to SUMO).

In summary: in three approaches (east/west/south) the occupancy error is **channel delay**, absorbed
by lag\* = 1.1 s (MAE@lag* 0.01–0.03 veh, exact 0.97–0.99); in **north it is residual counting error**
(MAE@lag* 0.254, exact 0.746) that lag* does not absorb, consistent with its lower detection fidelity
(far field / north entry).
